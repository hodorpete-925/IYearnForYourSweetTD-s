Gentleman, Players, Haters, and To Whom This May Concern,

The 2026 NFL season is upon us, and with it comes everything we hold near and dear to our hearts about the NFL season. That crisp beer you pour right before the one o'clock games on a beautiful fall Sunday. Maybe, if you're lucky, your wife grabbing you a fresh one from the fridge: "Don't worry honey, I'll take care of the kids, the laundry, and the errands today. You enjoy your little game with your silly friends." The rush of checking your score when a big play hits, watching your projected points climb while that lousy manager across from you falls further and further behind his.

The RedZone countdown, and that beautiful voice of Scott Hanson welcoming you to another Sunday glued to the couch trying to digest eight games at once. Octobox, we are here for it. Maybe there's something in the oven, maybe you've got a stellar bolognese stewing in a big pot (I know my boy Bill will), or buffalo sauce simmering on the stove tingling your nostrils as you prep to eat a dozen wings. Maybe you're waiting at the door for the Uber Eats guy with a zesty, cheesy pizza. You added pepperoni, maybe even sausage, because you deserve it. You've waited this long and you've arrived at another season of I Yearn For Your Sweet TD's.

It won't always be this easy. Maybe this is all just a fantasy I'm dreaming up in my head. There will be distractions. Dogs will need walking, diapers will need changing, kids will be bothering you, and wives will need attention, especially once they notice you've been on your phone for hours placing bets on the next slate because the five-leg parlay just died. Or maybe you got sucked into the vortex that is our league chat. Paul is arguing about how he's better, Tom is raging about the Pats, Brian is dropping a lame dad joke, and ODB just asked how many points a kicker gets when he scores a home run. Maybe you're just watching your projected score plummet and telling yourself the team can turn it around because you've still got the afternoon games, the evening game, and you sure as hell got MNF.

There's plenty to look forward to. Halloween is around the corner and that just means there are a thousand pumpkin beers we need to drink between now and then. Thanksgiving is one of the best days of the year to eat and drink your fill and yell at the TV when a world-class athlete slips on the turf at the goal line and costs you six points while you sit there with gravy on your shirt and the top button of your pants undone, screaming about the injustice that was just committed against you. I also look forward to those quiet weekends, it's getting colder outside, the sun is setting sooner, and there's really not much more to do other than carve out a little time for yourself to watch some football and track your fantasy team.

Bets will be placed. Waiver claims will be won, and waiver claims will be lost by a dollar. Trades will be proposed. Some of you are going to forget to respond after Tom sends you a dissertation worthy of Harvard on why you should take his deal. Trades will happen, we're geared for it. Some will go through, some will get countered to death, and we'll argue the whole way. Most importantly, we're going to have a great fucking time.

Good luck. Let's get the season going with some draft grades that will make you feel better than Yahoo ever has.

*The 2026 Draft Grades*

*1. Kowboy Hippie (Kyle W.) | Grade: C+*

Kyle swooped in this season and took over a roster Dan did his best to salvage, trading all summer and stacking a decent pile of picks. I really like Jordan Love. Without Josh Jacobs I think he has to throw more, and even if Jacobs does start, he's going to be weighed down by the ankle bracelet he has to wear while they figure out how to convict him for his crimes. Yikes. I also like Kyler Murray more and more this year. Arizona absolutely sucked for him and I think he turns a new leaf.

Not thrilled about the RBs. Jeremiyah Love is going to be expensive to keep, so he's probably a plug and play for the season unless he miraculously has an insane year. Swift is a solid and perhaps slightly underrated pick. Brooks and Rachaad White don't exactly get me out of bed in the morning.

Absolutely love the WRs on this team. Higgins, Waddle, Adams, Tet McMillan, and Worthy are a worthy stable for our rancher down in Texas. Goedert is the old bull aimlessly walking around the pasture. Likely could be the wolf prowling for sheep at the edge of the woods. Look at me, I did a ranch analogy.

**C+** on a team that Kyle has yet to really touch. Howdy and good luck, my friend.

*2. Are Bonita Fish Big? (Pete) | Grade: B-*

I'm always happy with how my team starts out. I'm an optimist. Felt that way last year and I finished in last place. Goes to show you how quickly this league can turn on you. Lamar Jackson was the obvious keeper for my QB1. He sucked last year relative to his cost, so let's hope for a rebound. Darnold should be serviceable to pretty good. I'll take it.

RB is thin for me, especially with Breece Hall coming off a groin injury in the preseason and Kyren Williams starting to sunset a bit and share the load with Blake Corum. I was super excited about Cam Skattebo until he broke his leg last year. Dude needs to stop eating crayons and get some calcium in his diet.

I'm excited about my WRs this year. I think this is finally the year Justin Herbert takes off, and with that I should see some nice dividends on Ladd McConkey. I tried selling him this offseason but too many managers had been burned by him in other leagues. Me too guys, and it sucks. Egbuka, Burden, and Stribling are super high upside guys. Terry McLaurin comes in as a nice veteran and I think the season goes better for him this year than it did last year.

Loving me some Loveland on this roster at $10. He could be the TE1 on the season.

Good starters, thin at the two spots that matter most in this league. **B-**.

*3. Dreorge Bledsoe (George) | Grade: B++*

Josh Allen is amazing. The dude puts the offense on his back with limited skill players and does it every year. He finally got some upgrades this offseason, and I'm excited about DJ Moore as a new weapon for him. I feel weird saying it, but I actually like Tyler Shough this year. The Saints went 5-4 after he took over, the schedule is soft, and there's upside where not many people are looking.

Javonte was a workhorse in Dallas last year and I love that he got a reset after Denver. Not crazy about Etienne or Montgomery, and the guys behind them are slim.

The receivers are the standouts: Olave, Flowers, Evans, BTJ, Odunze, Shakir. Evans is old and hurt again, but that dude can ball when he's on the field. Let's hope Andrews can round it out as a pass catcher and get back to the Lamar connection they had back in the day.

It would be an **A-** if it wasn't for the thin RBs. So I made it special and gave George a special **B++**. All praise Josh Allen's might.

*4. The Lady Boys (Bill) | Grade: A-*

Bill inherited a roster that looked weak at first, but upon closer examination he's done great for himself. He drafted three QBs, meaning he read the rules, unlike our boy Paul, who's still poring over the law of this league's land (this joke won't ever get old and I am happy to enlighten the newcomers). Aren't you a lawyer, Paul? Trevor Lawrence was a top-five QB last year and nobody wants to admit it. I had to double check that for myself. I really like Bo Nix, and I guess I can't fault Bill for drafting Kirk Cousins, but I think he's a waste of time.

The RBs here are stellar and why I think Bill is the dark horse of the season. Saquon, Price, and Jacobs are a crazy tag team for this roster. If Bill survives the 4-6 weeks Jacobs is out, he's in contention and Jacobs is going to be fresh.

Nico Collins is the best player on the team. You have to respect Rashee Rice and the Chiefs regardless of what Mahomes has been and is going through. Sutton worries me with Waddle in town, but Bill also rounds out the TEs with both Kyle Pitts and Tyler Warren. I guarantee that one gets traded by the deadline. KC Concepcion has been a really intriguing player for me this year. The talent is unquestionable; the Browns, unfortunately, are not. Regardless, I'm a sucker for rookies and I love the pickup.

**A-**. Bill did some good work in the draft and I like this team more and more the longer I look at it.

*5. ROOTIN' TUTEN' COWBOY (Monty) | Grade: A-*

Best team name in the league right now. Especially after Brian had that weird-ass tramp stamp of a team name that was the map coordinates to where we had our summit this year on the beach. We get it dude, you don't listen to instructions or check your texts. You showed up where we had the summit last year.

I'm really pulling for Jayden Daniels this year and I'm hoping he doesn't go the way of RG3. Mayfield is an excellent QB whether you like Tampa or not and will be a threat all season. Oh yeah, and Monty has Daniel Jones sitting pretty on the bench for whenever he needs him or just wants to leverage a trade. Might be the best QB tandem in the entire league.

Dart throws on Kaelon Black in SF and Coleman in Denver. I honestly like both of them a lot. Coleman could be what RJ Harvey has not amounted to, and I have a feeling SF is going to need to lean on its backup RBs this season. Tuten and Dobbins are fine starts, but again, like so many other rosters on here, RB just feels thin but more promising.

I'm excited about DJ Moore and I think he puts up better numbers than he did last year with Josh Allen now throwing him the ball. Garrett Wilson has to be better than last year because Geno Smith cannot possibly be worse than any QB the Jets started in 2025. I say this with little bias: Garrett Wilson is special. Quentin "Quge" Johnston should continue to improve, and again, I really think Justin Herbert picks it up this year behind a healthy O-line, even if that dude is simping for Madison Beer 24/7.

Bowers is the best tight end in football. Hope he gets healthy soon. When he does, watch out.

Solid team that deserves a strong **A-** because the QBs are going to deliver.

*6. High Priest Gabagool (Aric) | Grade: C-*

Five quarterbacks! Sixteen roster spots! Thirty percent of the team is QBs, and none of them are exciting in any way. Cam Ward, Mendoza, Shedeur, Deshaun Watson, and Bryce Young. I've been staring at this for a day and I still don't know which two he's starting.

Jonathan Taylor is the prize from the Jeanty deal and he's still a top-five back. Croskey-Merritt could win the Washington job outright and would be a steal if he does. MarShawn Lloyd is a coin flip, and Braelon Allen and Tracy are just okay. Might be some upside in there, but I'm not excited.

Justin Jefferson is Justin Jefferson. Malik Nabers worries me, but I wouldn't be able to resist keeping him either. Kelce... eh, I dunno man. T Swift has got him by the balls and I think he's lost his focus. Why can't he be as cool as his brother?

**C-**, and it should be worse, but I am generous. Five QBs!

*7. TheDarkKnight06 (Dan M.) | Grade: C*

According to every pundit this offseason, Caleb Williams is undeniable in his talent. Did you guys watch him on [Hot Ones](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qUsUCHxhYZg)? What a stud. If Cam Skattebo is chewing on crayons, then Jaxson Dart is sniffing rubber cement. Regardless, he's going to play hard this season, and maybe John Harbaugh is the only teacher in the class who can tame this young upstart. Love me the upside.

Dan targeted and ultimately landed KW III. Good for you. He's a great lease for the season and will be stellar. Have a plan for the second half of it, because I don't find Ken Walker to be that reliable. Bucky Irving, on the other hand, is still the back in Tampa and I like this for Dan. Gainwell, Aaron Jones, and Sean Tucker round it out. Gainwell has the highest upside.

Carnell Tate is intriguing, but it's all correlated to Cam Ward. Woof. Josh Downs, Matthew Golden, Tank Dell (Yay ODB! He might finally play for you!), and Alec Pierce round out this super thin WR group. I'm not a smart man but I think I see some holes here. I wish ODB the best.

LaPorta is a great value as a $10 keeper and that offense is going to flow in Detroit. Hunter Henry is a trade chip he should be shopping to somebody who needs a TE.

The QBs carry this team and not much else does. **C**.

*8. CeeDeez nuts (Alex) | Grade: B+*

McCaffrey and Gibbs. Wow. Say what you want about CMC, but you know the production is there, and Gibbs is generational talent. But wait, there's more! So is CMC. Juggernaut of an RB duo.

Lamb and DeVonta are the cornerstones and Lamb is going to have a great year. Pittman and Addison are nice stashes, with Addison the WR3 on this roster. I think him and Kyler put something fun together this year.

Everyone is hyping Fannin this year, but I just don't see anyone biting in any of the league drafts I have been a part of. He also plays for the Browns and nobody there is reliable.

Stroud scares me this year and I'm feeling off about him. Rodgers needs a new job. It's sad, but I think we've all been avoiding Pittsburgh talent this offseason.

Best RB room in the league, worst QB room in the league. **B+** because the RBs are gods amongst men in a thin RB year.

*9. Alice in First Down Chains (Tom) | Grade: A-*

Jalen Hurts and Patrick Mahomes at the helm of this team would have had us shit our pants a few years ago. Tom's rolling the dice on Mahomes, and I know he's got a raging boner in his pants for Hurts. These could be the QB2 and QB3 on the season, behind only Josh Allen.

If I were Tom, I would ride Derrick Henry into the ground. This is a player you keep betting on until you lose. I think he has a luxury some of us don't, because he's got Omarion Hampton and Jordan Mason to fill in when Henry falls down. Mason is flying under the radar and Hampton's got nothing but upside in front of him. Monangai has been a stash target for me in every league I'm in, and he keeps getting drafted before I get the chance. There's something to that, and Tom gets credit for it. I just can't dish it out for Charbonnet. I think Jadarian Price is the real deal, and Holani is hungry in Seattle.

The receivers are the best starting three anybody drafted: Nacua, AJ Brown, London. Behind them it's Boutte and Ja'Kobi Lane. I'm on the fence on AJ Brown, but the pedigree is there. Boutte to me has always been undervalued, and Ja'Kobi Lane is going to be a factor this year. Nacua and London are phenomenal players.

**A-**. The ceiling is as high as anyone's.

*10. Mr. McGibblets (Greg) | Grade: B*

Greg has great quarterbacks and weird running backs. Purdy is the value everyone overlooks every year, and I love that Greg snagged him and never let go. I feel like I gush over Herbert every single year. This is the year, guys. A healthy O-line is what he's needed and the Chargers appear to have delivered. Stafford won't score 46 TDs again, but who cares if he's your QB3?

The backfield is Stevenson, Dowdle, Hubbard, Harvey, Allgeier, and Woody Marks. Six backs and not one I really feel good about today. Will one of them step up and become an every week starter? Stranger things have happened, I know.

Christian Watson is a boom-or-bust WR2 with the Packers room finally thinned out. Marvin Harrison Jr. needs to arrive at some point. Michael Wilson and Stefon Diggs are depth that carry more upside than some would admit. Kincaid is a tight end. On the Buffalo Bills. Better than most flyers on TE outside of the top 3.

Good quarterbacks. The rest is up in the air. Greg will surprise you this year, mark my words. **B**.

*11. Briguy's Strange Boutte (Paul) | Grade: B+*

Paul has four quarterbacks on paper, legally bound to play for his team until terminated otherwise. Dak and Goff are what matters. Penix and Carson Beck are irrelevant.

Bijan to me is somehow a forgotten elite RB. I've never drafted him. I've never really watched an Atlanta Falcons game (I have a life). I'm somehow not swayed in any positive direction by him. I'm sure the fantasy stats will be there, and I know he's an undeniable talent. He's no Gibbs, and he certainly has not accomplished what CMC has. Congrats on the $200 you spent, Paul. I'm sure it will amount to relevance, but I don't know how Bijan keeps up on this garbage Atlanta team. On the flip side, Henderson and Judkins are exciting. I think Stevenson falls flat this year in favor of Henderson, eventually. Judkins is only going to get better. Some advice: sell high, buy low.

Amon-Ra is elite. Amon-Ra plays for Detroit. Detroit is elite? Remember when Detroit was a bad team? I honestly don't anymore. I love that offense. Parker Washington has been blowing up in rankings this offseason. It's all tied to Trevor Lawrence, obviously. Apply your grain of salt as you see fit. Meyers, Shaheed, Tre Tucker, and Wan'Dale are volume plays. I will say I agree with Paul on this one take: Tucker Kraft is a top TE and I hope he gets back into what he started last year.

**B+**. Top-heavy, and the top is real.

*12. Two Mugs But Zero Hugs (Brian) | Grade: A*

We all talk about Brian enough all season after he's won two championships in a row. We know he has a great team. He appears invincible, but he's also susceptible to kryptonite.

Joe Burrow and Drake Maye are two starting QBs any of us would love to have in this format. Joe Burrow has had his challenges finishing out an NFL season, and honestly, Drake Maye isn't proven in my eyes. Sure, Maye made it to a Super Bowl. So did Russell Wilson. Where's he at these days?

Ja'Marr Chase, JSN, and George Pickens are legit WR talent. That's a stellar group and Brian has worked hard to solidify this core. There's really no color I can add. We're jealous.

Cook and Achane are both amazing talents. You know what James Cook can do, and you know how I feel about the Bills and Josh Allen. Achane is a bit more interesting. I think he has the talent, but I don't think the Dolphins offense can offer him a platform for what he can do. When Tua was out, Achane started to lose his consistency. Is Malik Willis the answer? Who cares, because Jeanty is also sitting there and I think he picks the Raiders up this year.

I'll say it again: this roster is clogged by two additional QBs that just don't make sense to me given the caliber of the rest of it. Two QBs that now share a Dolphins lineage isn't exactly inspiring, and that's wasted opportunity cost in my eyes.

It's almost an insult to us, but Brian rounds this roster out with McBride at TE and some high upside in Blake Corum, Jordyn Tyson, and Makai Lemon.

A reluctant **A** grade here only because it's Brian.
