Gentleman,

Keeper season deadline is almost here. Reminder that we had an oopsie last year and inadvertently went to 6 bench spots vs the original 5. We are reverting back to 5. Let's review some of the keeper decisions we are going to be making heading into the deadline.

PSA: Deadline is Saturday, August 29th at 11:59 PM. The deadline to get trades in is 24 hours before the deadline. The 48-hour counter gets truncated for anything that happens within 48 hours of the deadline. The counter deadline is going to be set at 8 PM on Saturday.

And the trade market has been open for business all summer. The Jeanty-for-Taylor swap headlined the off-season, and the deals kept coming: Dan shipped Olave to George for Waddle, sent a 4th to Malco for Tetairoa McMillan and a 12th, I bought Scary Terry from Scott, and Greg and Brian swapped $10 backs. Everything below reflects all of it, with traded players carrying their frozen trade-time DRC.

Let's get into it!



*1. Fear the Peel (Dan Vescuso) | Market Cap: $510*

Dan won the lottery, picks first in every round, and carries the cheapest roster in the league at $510. He spent the spring hoarding picks, three extra 7ths and now a pair of 12ths, then spent the off-season actually using them: Olave out for Waddle, and a 4th to Malco for Tetairoa McMillan. Don't hate the trade, but watch who you do business with Dan.

*Steals: *

- Jordan Love came in as QB13 last year in our league and $30 to keep. Great player, phenomenal value.
- D'Andre Swift is the same story at RB14 last year in our league but somehow I'm just not that excited about this player. What do I know, I came in last place.
- Tetairoa McMillan looks like he's a steal here costing Dan just $60. I think he's a bit hyped up this year and I have some concerns on who's throwing him the ball. Regardless, it worked out last year and Tet came in as the WR13 on the year. You let me know if you think he's going to be able to run it back again.

*Big money: *

- Jaylen Waddle was a nice trade IMO. Waddle should be a beast on Denver. Unlike Olave, Waddle gets hurt and just keeps on playing. Meanwhile, Olave is one head injury away from being out for a prolonged amount of time. I liked the move and thought it was pretty savvy.
- Big money price tag on Davante Adams, big time decision. Kyler Murray, well he's just a smaller decision...

*$10 gold: *

- Jake Ferguson had himself a top-5 TE season in our league sandwiched just between Brock Bowers (TE4) and Tyler Warren (TE6). For $10 he's free money at the thinnest position on the board.
- Kenneth Gainwell and Marcus Mariota, sitting in the $10 pile, quietly outscored a couple of the real keeper decisions above them.



*2. Are Bonita Fish Big? (Pete Hodor) | Market Cap: $670*

I went big at the end of the season and stockpiled as many cheap upside players as I could off the waiver wire. Waiver wire is huge in this league and what else are you going to do when you're in last place? I even went as far as dropping Geno Smith, a rare QB asset to hit the waiver wire in this league. I say this as a Jets fan, I don't see anything in Geno Smith.

*Steals: *

- Kyren Williams came in as RB8 last year in our league and costs me $10. I've been riding his production for a few years now. Does he sink down some more with Blake Corum coming into the mix? Who cares, he's $10.
- Sam Darnold gave me a QB14 season and costs $30 to keep in a two-QB league. It makes all that much sweeter that he helped beat the Pats in the SB. Proud of him for moving on from the Jets dumpster fire of an organization and finding success. Sorry to all my Pats fans in this league. Ladd McConkey at $30 is the same story at WR23. Clean values.

*Big money: *

- Lamar Jackson, the bill keeps coming in each year just like my NFL Sunday Ticket subscription. I hope paying for Lamar makes me as happy as NFL Sunday Ticket. QB21 last year screams buy the dip and re-up. Fingers crossed for success because my team hinges on Lamar doing well. It did last year at least.
- Terry McLaurin is my off-season buy from Scott, $60 to keep off a down year. The bet is on the down year, not the player.
- Matthew Golden at 54 points last season and $50 to keep is not a sentence I enjoy typing. Especially because I sent a pick to Vesco to get him. Ouch.

*$10 gold: *

- Colston Loveland and Luther Burden feel like a $10 lottery ticket I found on the ground, it was already scratched off, and it's a winner!



*3. Dreorge Bledsoe (George Mensing) | Market Cap: $890*

George's ledger is a tale of two contracts: the easiest $200 in the league and the hardest one. $890 to keep it all (he won't), picking third.

*Steals: *

- Chris Olave is the return in the Waddle swap, $30 to keep off a WR6 season in our league. My injury concerns stand, but that's tempered with what everyone is saying about Tyler Shough. Stay healthy my man.
- Javonte Williams came in as RB11 and costs $30. Boring, but a weekly start, and boring at $30 is a compliment. He did fade hard in the second half of the year.
- Zay Flowers at WR12 for $80 is fair value, and Baltimore just extended him, so somebody in the building agrees.

*Big money: *

- Josh Allen is the easiest $200 anyone spends this month. QB1 by a mile and the top quarterback in four of the last six seasons. Sign the check.
- Breece Hall is the same $200 for last year's RB17. Supremely talented back, wrong offense, and the new Jets staff keeps talking about a three-back rotation. Fuck the Jets suck.
- James Conner scored 27.3 points and costs $60. Oh boy.

*$10 gold: *

- J.J. McCarthy is $10 insurance on the Minnesota job that Kyler Murray was signed to take from him. Cheap enough to watch the fight?
- The Texans DST scored 236 and outscored half the skill players on this roster. George remains the league's DST whisperer (from Claude not me, interesting take).



*4. The Lady Boys (Bill Keenan) | Market Cap: $760*

Someone please change Bill's team name. Regardless, the twelfth chair is filled: welcome Bill Keenan, who takes over The Lady Boys and inherits the fourth pick plus one of the more interesting keep menus in the league. Nothing like making $200 keeper calls in your first week on the job.

*Steals: *

- Nico Collins came in as WR8 in our league and costs just $10. Everyone seems to love him even more than the finish suggests, and he did it with backup quarterbacks throwing to him. Welcome to the league, Bill, here's your signing bonus.
- Bo Nix at QB8 for $60 in a two-QB league, and Denver spent the offseason buying him more weapons. The easiest yes on Bill's new menu.
- Rashee Rice costs $10 while the market calls him a fourth rounder. That gap is the whole pitch.

*Big money: *

- Trevor Lawrence gave a real QB5 season and costs the full $200, but nine rushing touchdowns did a lot of that scoring and nobody expects nine again.
- Saquon Barkley costs $200 for the RB13 version. Which story do you believe: 600-plus touches over two years at age 29, or a down year caused by the line and the scheme?
- Isiah Pacheco at $100. He's in Detroit explicitly to back up Gibbs.

*$10 gold: *

- Courtland Sutton came in as WR9 and costs $10, but it was a touchdown mirage, he was 20th in points per game, and Denver just imported Waddle to be the new number one.



*5. JUST THE TUA US (Scott Montgomery) | Market Cap: $530*

Scott's roster is a case study in the two ends of the DRC curve: one giant bill at the top and a $10 pile that could start for half the league. After selling Scary Terry, he's down to $530 to keep it all, the second-cheapest book in the league, picking fifth.

*Steals: *

- Chase Brown came in as RB10 and costs $10, and the season was better than the finish. Once the backfield was his he was a top-five back the rest of the way, and Cincinnati added nobody behind him.
- Baker Mayfield put up 294.7 points and costs $10.
- Daniel Jones came in as QB15 and costs $10. Indy rehabilitated him and Monty reaps the rewards.

*Big money: *

- Jayden Daniels is the hardest call in the league. 128 points last year at the full $200, but the down year was three unrelated injuries. Healthy he could be worth it.
- Aaron Jones at RB42 and $80 to keep. The mileage is on the odometer.

*$10 gold: *

- Geno Smith is in the pile too. Scott's entire QB room costs less than one of Tom's keepers. LOL!
- Bhayshul Tuten joins the pile from my roster in the McLaurin deal. I think Monty is better at trades and moves than both Bri-guy and Tom combined.



*6. High Priest Gabagool 🤌🏻 (Aric Tao) | Market Cap: $870*

Tizzle got himself Jonathan Taylor and did so by trading away Jeanty who may or may not be down for an extended period. Love it when this happens and a case study for you guys pulling trades so early. Remember when Brian traded Gibbs away for Achane in Feb the week after the SB? Weird. I digress, the rest of Aric's menu is a study in paying up for belief. $870 to keep it all, picking sixth.

*Steals: *

- Jonathan Taylor, the trade already reads right. RB2 in our league last year and $100 to keep, with the safest volume profile in football. The monster stretch came with Daniel Jones upright. When the Colts get to the goal line, the ball is going to Taylor.
- Jameson Williams came in as WR11 and costs $30, four rounds ahead of the market. Good quiet value there.

*Big money: *

- Justin Jefferson is the $200 question. The down year was entirely quarterback play, and Minnesota went and signed Kyler. The whole fantasy community is chanting "buy the dip."
- Malik Nabers at $100 off a torn ACL is scary, but the August news is encouraging and the coach calls Week 1 a reasonable bet.
- Brandon Aubrey is a $30 kicker. K1 by a distance, sure, but a $30 kicker? Aric has done crazier things.

*$10 gold: *

- Bryce Young put up 239.5 points and is sitting in the $10 pile. I guess?

*9. The Prince of Darkness (Tom Watson) | Market Cap: $1,350*

The most expensive book in the league: $1,350 to keep it all, with four separate $200 lines before anyone else keeps a soul. He picks ninth and has been stockpiling late-round picks from Vescuso like sandbags.

*Steals: *

- Puka Nacua, WR1 overall, ten dollars. Glad you found a way to save some money, Tom.
- Omarion Hampton at $100 is one where the finish lies. He broke his ankle midseason, was a top-16 back per game, and now Mike McDaniel is calling Chargers plays.
- Derrick Henry came in as RB7 and costs $100 against a top-13 market. Father Time remains undefeated, but so far, so is Henry.

*Big money: *

- TreVeyon Henderson costs the full $200 off an RB20 rookie year.
- Patrick Mahomes tore his ACL in Week 15 after running as the overall QB1. With Hurts also at full freight, that's $400 locked into two QB spots, one of them on crutches in January.
- CeeDee Lamb's down year was three missed games and a three-touchdown season, not a decline, and the touchdowns went to, awkwardly, George Pickens, who costs Brian thirty bucks. The name holds value but is he worth keeping?

*$10 gold: *

- See: Nacua, Puka.



*10. Mr. McGibblets (Greg Pearson) | Market Cap: $810*

Greg is holding both the worst contract-to-production line in the league and the best one, which feels very on brand.

*Steals: *

- Matthew Stafford: 399.7 points, QB2 overall, an MVP season, thirty dollars. Yes he's 38 and the touchdowns will regress, but he can give back a dozen of them and still demolish this price. Greg wins the value column this year, and I could not be prouder of my boy Gregor.
- Travis Kelce at TE3 for $50 in whatever year of the farewell tour this is.

*Big money: *

- Garrett Wilson costs $200 off a WR49 season. The knee ended his year in December, the target share before it was real, and Geno Smith is the best quarterback he's ever had which is sad.
- Tee Higgins has 21 touchdowns over the last two years and no full season since 2022.
- Alvin Kamara scored 84 points, costs $30.

*$10 gold: *

- Woody Marks arrives from Brian in the Corum swap and immediately becomes one of the better $10 pieces here.
- Rico Dowdle is the best depth stash nobody is talking about, and Rhamondre Stevenson, the entire reason Tom's Henderson keep is a debate, is lurking for ten bucks.



*11. Briguy's Strange Boutte (Paul Lewis) | Market Cap: $1,100*

Four max contracts and an $1,100 keeper cost, the second biggest in the league.

*Steals: *

- Quinshon Judkins came in as RB23 and costs $30 with the market two rounds ahead of the cost. Clean value.
- Stefon Diggs gave WR17 production. A rare case where I'd trust the prior season over the boards. At $50 it's close.

*Big money: *

- Josh Jacobs is the fourth $200 line. Tough, but Paul likes to flex his checkbook.
- Dak Prescott at $200 for a QB4 season is a fine keep, which I am sure Paul will confirm after checking a rulebook from a different league.
- Bijan Robinson and Amon-Ra St. Brown are the defensible max contracts: 41 percent of Atlanta's total yardage, and a top-3 receiver three years running. Four $200 lines on one ledger is still a choice.

*$10 gold: *

- The pile features two DSTs and a kicker scoring like flex players. If anyone does it, it will be Paul.



*12. 42°19'50.3"N 71°01'52.0"W (Brian Malconian) | Market Cap: $820*

Brian picks last and might not care, because this menu is obnoxious. The champ opened the month trading for Jeanty (injury alert!), then spent the off-season selling from strength: McMillan and a 12th to Vesco for a 4th, and Marks to Greg for Corum.

*Steals: *

- Take your pick of larcenies: Jaxon Smith-Njigba was the WR2 overall and costs $80, James Cook won the rushing title at $50, George Pickens was a no-fluke WR4 at $30, and De'Von Achane, the most consistent back in fantasy, is $30 strapped to Miami's dumpster fire. At some point the league office should open an investigation led by Inspector Tom "Brown" Watson.
- Drake Maye came in as QB3 overall and costs $30, and New England just handed him A.J. Brown. The best keep-per-dollar line this side of Puka.
- Trey McBride, TE1 overall, ten dollars. What is worse than a felony?

*Big money: *

- Joe Burrow at 141 points and $200 is the scary half; the market still calling him a top-8 pick is the other. He's healthy, with the easiest schedule in football for his receivers, and that offense is nearly unstoppable when he's upright.
- Ashton Jeanty has some developing news going on and I honestly wouldn't be surprised if Brian gets cold feet and drops him at the keeper deadline. Keep your eyes peeled!

*$10 gold: *

- Blake Corum arrives from Greg at $10, which means the champ now owns the other half of the argument over my Kyren keep. Menace.
- Tua headlines the pile, and Brian also owns Malik Willis, the man now personally responsible for Achane's ceiling.
